TABC License Application Tracker — New Filings Every Week

Before a restaurant or bar opens in Texas, it almost always files a TABC license application first — sometimes months before construction finishes or a sign goes up. OpeningWire tracks these applications from the moment they're filed, checking Texas's public TABC pending-applications feed daily and surfacing new filings the same week they appear. That's weeks or months of lead time before a Restaurant Activity Report or a search engine would ever surface the location, because those only catch a venue once its license is fully issued. For suppliers who want the earliest possible signal — before a competitor even knows the account exists — this is the page. Applications that stall out — withdrawn, denied, or simply abandoned — are checked periodically and dropped from the feed, so what you see is a live tracker of applications still moving toward a license, not a growing pile of dead leads.

Frequently asked questions

How early does this catch a new restaurant?

At the point the TABC application is filed — typically weeks to months before the license is issued and long before opening day.

Do you track applications that get withdrawn or denied?

Yes. Tracked applications are periodically re-checked against the public feed, and ones that are no longer live are removed rather than staying in the feed as a stale lead.
