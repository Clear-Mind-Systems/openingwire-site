Frequently asked questions

Does an application confirm a restaurant will open?

No. An application shows that a business has sought a liquor license. It does not establish an opening date or guarantee that the application will be approved.

Does this sample confirm an application is still pending?

No. These are sample records, and their current status may have changed. Check the refresh information above and verify the application in current TABC records.
