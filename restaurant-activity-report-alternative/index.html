A Faster Alternative to TABC's Restaurant Activity Report

The Texas Alcoholic Beverage Commission publishes its own Restaurant Activity Report, a free public tool for browsing recently licensed retailers. It's a genuinely useful starting point — and also raw: every license type mixed together, no restaurant-versus-bar classification, no filtering out off-premise retail or closed applications, and no way to have it delivered to you automatically. OpeningWire is built from the same public TABC filings the Restaurant Activity Report draws on, with the layer distributors, POS resellers, and hospitality suppliers actually need on top: venues classified into restaurant, bar/club, or retail; deduplicated to one row per real premises rather than one row per license; pending applications tracked alongside issued licenses; and the whole feed delivered as a CSV every Monday morning — no manual pulls, no re-filtering the same report every week.
