Frequently asked questions

What sources does OpeningWire use?

OpeningWire identifies restaurant and bar leads from public Texas Alcoholic Beverage Commission (TABC) liquor-license applications and issuances. These records do not cover every restaurant or establish a venue's opening date.

What does the subscription include?

Weekly CSV feeds organize restaurant and bar leads by territory using TABC applications and issued-license records, with venue categories, location details, and record dates. See the homepage for the features included in each plan.

How much does it cost?

One-metro plans start at $99 per month. See pricing on the homepage for plan details.
