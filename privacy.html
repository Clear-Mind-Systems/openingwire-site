What we collect

When you subscribe or contact us we collect your name, email address, company, and billing information. Payment details are processed and stored by Stripe — we never see or store full card numbers. Our website does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

How we use it

To deliver reports you subscribed to, process billing, respond to support requests, and send service-related emails. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing.

About the data in our reports

Our product contains information about businesses (venue names, business addresses, license types and dates) sourced from public government records. It is business information from public sources, not consumer personal data.

Account information is retained while your subscription is active and for tax/accounting purposes afterward. Email support@openingwire.com to request deletion of your account data.
