TABC liquor-license applications and issuances can help suppliers identify Texas restaurants and bars to research. Frequently asked questions

What is a pre-opening restaurant lead?

For prospecting, a TABC application or issued license can be a signal to investigate a business. The record alone does not confirm whether it has opened, when it will open, or whether it has chosen vendors.

How fresh is this data?

The subscription is weekly, with delivery scheduled for Mondays. The sample on this page has its own refresh status, shown above. Record dates describe licensing activity; they do not show when this page was updated.
