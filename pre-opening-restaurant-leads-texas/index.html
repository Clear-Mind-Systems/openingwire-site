Pre-Opening Restaurant Leads in Texas — Updated Every Monday

Every week, new restaurants and bars across Texas file for or receive their TABC liquor licenses — public filings that surface weeks or months before a location opens its doors, hires staff, or places its first orders. For food and beverage distributors, POS resellers, kitchen equipment dealers, insurance agents, and marketing agencies that sell into the hospitality industry, each of these filings is a pre-opening restaurant lead: a real business, at a real address, that has not yet chosen its vendors. OpeningWire turns Texas's public TABC data into a weekly lead feed built specifically for B2B sales into new restaurants and bars — filtered to sellable categories, classified by type, deduplicated to one row per premises, and delivered by email every Monday for the Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, Austin, and San Antonio metros, with statewide coverage available.
