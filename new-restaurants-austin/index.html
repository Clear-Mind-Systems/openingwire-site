Austin Restaurant & Bar Leads from TABC Records

OpeningWire helps suppliers research restaurants and bars in the Austin metro through public Texas Alcoholic Beverage Commission (TABC) liquor-license applications and issuances. The weekly subscription includes leads from these records, and the sample below shows license records for the metro. A filing or issued license is a reason to investigate a business; it does not confirm an opening date, operating status, or purchasing plans. Restaurants that do not apply for a liquor license are outside this source's coverage.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these Austin restaurant and bar leads come from?

OpeningWire identifies restaurant and bar leads from public Texas Alcoholic Beverage Commission (TABC) liquor-license applications and issuances. These records do not cover every restaurant or establish a venue's opening date.

How often is this page updated?

The subscription is weekly, with delivery scheduled for Mondays. The sample on this page has its own refresh status, shown above. Record dates describe licensing activity; they do not show when this page was updated.

I sell to restaurants — how do I get the weekly feed?

OpeningWire offers a weekly CSV subscription with restaurant and bar leads from TABC applications and issued licenses for your selected territory. One-metro plans start at $99 per month; see the pricing section on the homepage for plan details.
