FAQ

Where does the data come from?

Texas Alcoholic Beverage Commission liquor-license applications and issuances. We aggregate, deduplicate, classify, and track these public records. This source does not cover every restaurant or establish a separate business-filings feed.

Who is this for?

Anyone who sells to new restaurants and bars: food and beverage distributors, POS and payments resellers, kitchen equipment dealers, payroll and insurance reps, marketing agencies, and suppliers of all kinds.

How fresh is it?

Subscriptions provide weekly feeds, with Monday delivery scheduled. The public sample has its own refresh status and provenance, shown above. Record dates do not establish when the feed was updated. Request an available sample to assess it.

What format do I get?

A clean CSV (name, category, stage, address, city, county, dates) plus a readable summary. Drops straight into any CRM or spreadsheet.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — subscriptions are month-to-month with self-serve cancellation. No contracts, no calls.

Do you cover states besides Texas?

The current offering covers Texas, with Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, Austin, and San Antonio metro plans. Multi-Territory includes a statewide summary.
